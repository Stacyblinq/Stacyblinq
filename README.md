## Ebere Anastasia Akpan

### **Cybersecurity Analyst | SOC Analyst | IT Support | Cloud Security (AWS)**

Result-driven Cybersecurity Professional with hands-on experience in Security Operations Center monitoring, threat detection, incident response, vulnerability assessment, cloud security, and IT support. I developed a strong interest in cybersecurity after recognizing how vulnerable individuals and small businesses in my community are to cyber threats. Growing up in Nigeria, I witnessed firsthand how limited digital awareness exposes people to fraud, data breaches, and online exploitation. This realization inspired me to pursue a career dedicated to protecting digital assets and promoting cybersecurity awareness.
I am deeply committed to supporting organizations and individuals build resilience against evolving cyber threats through proactive security practices, education, and risk management. Beyond cybersecurity, I am passionate about empowering the next generation of technology professionals and have participated in initiatives that encourage young girls to explore careers in technology.
My long-term goal is to contribute to strengthening digital security systems, advancing cybersecurity awareness, and supporting the development of a safer digital ecosystem in Nigeria and beyond.
